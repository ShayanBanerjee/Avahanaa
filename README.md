# Avahanaa

A QR sticker on your windshield that lets anyone reach you about your vehicle —
without ever revealing your phone number.

Park in Bangalore and things happen you cannot see. Your car blocks someone in
and they get angry enough to damage it. A cop is about to tow it. A passer-by
notices a broken window or your lights left on. Avahanaa gives that person a way
to reach you in seconds: they scan the sticker with Google Lens or any camera —
no app install — pick a reason, and your phone alerts you. You get moving. They
see that you are on the way and calm down. Neither side learns anything about
the other.

Live on Google Play. Flutter app, Firebase backend.

## Setup

```bash
flutter pub get
flutter run
```

Firebase config (`lib/firebase_options.dart`, `android/app/google-services.json`)
is generated by FlutterFire and already committed. Release signing needs
`android/key.properties`, which is intentionally not in git.

```bash
flutter analyze
flutter test
flutter build appbundle --release
```

Build-time overrides via `--dart-define`: `QR_REDIRECT_HOST`,
`QR_REDIRECT_PATH`, `QR_SHORT_ROUTE_PREFIX`, `QR_USE_SHORT_ROUTE`.

## Where things are

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `lib/screens/` | UI — auth, home, notifications, profile, QR |
| `lib/services/` | Firestore, auth, FCM, notification routing |
| `lib/utils/` | QR payload builder, Indian plate validator |
| `docs/` | Architecture, contracts, and design decisions |
| `CLAUDE.md` | Full project context for AI-assisted development |

The QR scan page and the FCM sender live outside this repo, at `avahanaa.com`.
The contract between them is in `docs/backend_contract.md`.
